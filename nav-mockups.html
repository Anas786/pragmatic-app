Inactive tabs collapse to icon-only circles. The active tab expands to icon + label with a spring morph. Same content, far less visual weight on the strip. This was the v2 TabSelector that crashed — see safe-path note below.

Pros Most visually distinctive. Fits more tabs on screen at once. Strong "what's selected" affordance.
Cons Original impl used Reanimated LinearTransition on 7 chips → commit-during-commit SIGABRT. Needs a re-architecture to ship.
Safe path to ship Drop layout=LinearTransition on the chips. Drive a single shared progress shared value from active-tab state; animate a single overlay "blob" that translates + resizes across the strip. Chips themselves stay static — no per-chip Reanimated. One worklet total, not seven.

Pill strip stays — pills become a position indicator. You can tap or swipe horizontally to switch. Subtle dot row at the bottom hints at gesture availability. Active tab auto-centers in the strip.

Pros Gesture-first, no functionality moves, ~1 day to implement, all tabs stay accessible.
Cons Doesn't reduce the "7 things at one level" cognitive load.
